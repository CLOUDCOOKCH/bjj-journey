Everything You Need

Built specifically for BJJ practitioners who want to take their training seriously.

Training Dashboard

Real-time stats at a glance - total sessions, monthly progress, current streak, and a training volume chart.

Interactive Calendar

Visual calendar with color-coded session types, mat time tracking, and one-click session logging.

Training Journal

Log every session with notes, intensity levels, duration, and session type. Filter and search your history.

Technique Library

Document techniques with YouTube links and personal notes. Build your own reference library over time.

Belt Progression

Visual belt timeline from white to black. Track stripe milestones and keep a full promotion history.

Achievements

Celebrate belt promotions, stripe awards, and training milestones. Set monthly goals and track your consistency.

See It in Action

A sleek dark-mode interface designed for focus and clarity.

Training Calendar

See your training at a glance with an interactive monthly calendar. Color-coded session types, mat time tracking, and the ability to quickly add past sessions make it easy to stay on top of your schedule.

Training Journal

Every session tells a story. Log session type, intensity, duration, techniques, and personal notes. Filter by type or search your entire history to find exactly what you need.

Progress Tracking

Visualize your journey from white to black belt. Track stripe progress, complete milestone checklists, and look back at your full promotion history as motivation to keep pushing forward.
